⚡ REGULATORY DECISION — The CLARITY Act failed
On September 15 the Senate rejected cloture on the motion to proceed to H.R. 3633, the Digital Asset Market CLARITY Act, 49–50. Sixty votes were required. All Democrats plus four Republicans — Collins, Hawley, Moran and Tillis — voted no. Republican leaders had released a revised bill on Sunday adding ethics restrictions aimed at Democratic concerns about officials profiting from crypto ventures; it wasn't enough. This effectively ends market-structure legislation in the Senate for 2026. Galaxy Digital's Mike Novogratz: "18 months of work between our industry, Democrats, and Republicans, and Clarity falls apart on the 5-yard line." Former CFTC chair Giancarlo's read: the SEC and CFTC will now write crypto rules by regulation instead of statute. Coinbase and Circle led a crypto-equity selloff.
